I'm a second-year PhD student in Computer Science at the Machine Learning Genoa Center (MaLGa), University of Genoa, advised by Prof. Luca Calatroni. My research focuses on learned regularisation, inverse problems, and uncertainty quantification for computational microscopy imaging — in particular image deconvolution for Image Scanning Microscopy (ISM) and fluorescence microscopy.

Away from the lab, I'm hopelessly fond of piano music and singing, and I love sports — pretty much every one under the sun.
